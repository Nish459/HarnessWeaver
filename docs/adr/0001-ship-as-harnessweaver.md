# ADR-0001: Ship as HarnessWeaver

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Ship as **HarnessWeaver**: `harnessweaver`, `harnessweaver-cli` (command `harnessweaver`), `harnessweaver-sidecar`, first-party plugins `harnessweaver-<name>`, `@harnessweaver/core`, `@harnessweaver/<name>`.

## Why

`harnesskit` is taken on PyPI and npm; the new names were free when checked (2026-10-17).
